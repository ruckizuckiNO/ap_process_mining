ZNB1: from END_WAIT_GR to posting

ZNB1 invoices that waited in SAP's goods-receipt check until the wait ended without a goods receipt (END_WAIT_GR), which sends them to the manual control queue (CTRL1). Here their clock runs from END_WAIT_GR to the first posting, and they are compared with the invoices sent straight to CTRL1 at validation.

387 invoices 174 POs 49 suppliers comparison: 5,610 straight to CTRL1

  1. One invoice = one PO_ID × SAP_PO_No; events ordered by timestamp, then Event_ID.
  2. Included: ZNB1 invoices with a Wait for goods receipt event whose SAP_Object_Code is END_WAIT_GR and no WAIT_GR event, that were posted. Every END_WAIT_GR event sets the routing code to CTRL1 (100%), so these invoices all end up in the manual queue. By route at validation: WAITGR 314, ZWMGRW 58, CTRL1 15.
  3. Clock start: the invoice's first END_WAIT_GR event, the moment the goods-receipt wait ended and accounts payable could act. 5 invoices have more than one; the first one counts.
  4. Clock stop: the first posting (MM Posted Automatically, Posted manually, MM Autopost after manual process). Days = stop − start in seconds / 86,400, calendar days.
  5. Late = more than 10 (or 30) days. Missed discount = 2% of the PO value split evenly over the PO's posted invoices, for each late invoice.
  6. Comparison group: ZNB1 invoices routed to CTRL1 at their first validation, posted, clock from arrival (Data management) to first posting. That report (Invoices sent straight to the manual queue) uses the invoice clock, which starts at goods receipt for the 1% that later get one, so its share over 10 days is slightly lower. The 15 END_WAIT_GR invoices that were sent to CTRL1 at validation and only later went through the goods-receipt check are in both groups.

7What the END_WAIT_GR event is

All 445 END_WAIT_GR events in ZNB1 are logged by User1587 (100% by a system user). They fall on weekdays only (0% at weekends), 80% between 07:00 and 17:59, with the most at 14:00. That fits either a scheduled job or a clerk releasing the wait in the invoice cockpit under the system's name; the log can't tell which.

Either way, until END_WAIT_GR the invoice was held in SAP's goods-receipt check, not in the manual queue. Starting the clock there treats these invoices the same way as the ones with a WAIT_GR event: the wait for the goods doesn't count against accounts payable.

9Limits

  • END_WAIT_GR is not a goods-receipt date. It marks when the invoice left the goods-receipt check; whether the goods had arrived by then is unknown.
  • The comparison group's clock starts at arrival. Under the assumption that suppliers invoice after delivery, that's fair; otherwise it includes some goods wait (see the direct-CTRL1 report).
  • Small group: 387 invoices, so supplier and month figures rest on few cases.
  • Invoice values are estimated by splitting the PO value evenly over its posted invoices.
