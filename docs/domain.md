# Domain reference: PO types and activities

Summarised from the case description. Counts are events in the event log.

## PO types

Every PO type belongs to one of five procurement situations. ZINS (Service order Type 1) and ZSER (Service order Type 2) are described in the case but do not occur in the event log.

| Code | Name | Situation | Events | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ZJD1 | Normal order | Standard purchasing | 40,241 | Goods bought for stock in warehouses or stores, then resold. The traditional supply chain. |
| ZNB1 | Manual order | Standard purchasing | 50,963 | Created by a purchaser who decides what to buy (e.g. extra consoles before Christmas, urgent spare parts). |
| ZJD3 | Automatic order | Standard purchasing | 10,788 | Generated by SAP when stock falls below a replenishment threshold. No purchaser involved. |
| ZNB2 | Third-party order Type 1 | Third-party (dropshipping) | 387,113 | Supplier ships directly to the customer; the retailer never handles the goods. |
| ZNB3 | Third-party order Type 2 | Third-party (dropshipping) | 87,549 | Same model; the difference from ZNB2 is company-specific (e.g. domestic vs international suppliers, or product category). |
| ZNB4 | Third-party order Type 3 | Third-party (dropshipping) | 135,226 | Same model, e.g. for bulky goods (large fridges, TVs, kitchens) that are costly to store. |
| ZNB6 | Third-party order Type 4 | Third-party (dropshipping) | 5,493 | Same model, e.g. supplier manufactures, delivers and installs; the retailer only sells and supports. |
| ZNBF | Transportation | Transportation | 271 | Logistics services (transport capacity, home delivery), not goods. |
| NB2 | Return to vendor | Returns | 171,223 | "Reverse PO": goods already bought through another PO type are sent back, e.g. damaged units; the supplier may issue a credit note. |
| ZRPC | Return order Type 1 | Returns | 1,616 | Specialised returns (failed quality checks, transport damage, wrong deliveries, warranty), kept separate for reporting and control. |
| ZRPM | Return order Type 2 | Returns | 47 | Another return variant, e.g. commercial returns (cancellations, overstock, pricing disputes) vs operational ones. |

The exact difference between ZNB2/3/4/6, and between ZRPC and ZRPM, depends on the retailer's SAP configuration and is not documented.

## Activities

Invoices are processed in Invoice Cockpit. MM = SAP Materials Management (PO-related invoices, matched against PO and goods receipt). FI = SAP Financial Accounting (non-PO invoices that need GL coding or cost allocation). WM = Warehouse Management.

**Intake and validation**
- *Initial validation*: first stage; automated checks that supplier, invoice number, PO reference, dates and amounts are present and valid.
- *Data management*: invoice master or transaction data is processed, reviewed, corrected or maintained.

**Routing**
- *Transfer to MM*: identified as PO-related and routed to MM for matching.
- *Transfer to FI*: routed to FI, typically because it is not linked to a PO.
- *MM for Manual processing after initial check*: belongs in purchasing but could not auto-post (discrepancy, missing info, exception); needs a purchasing or AP specialist.
- *FI for Manual processing after initial check*: in FI but needs manual review or correction, often incomplete coding.

**Goods receipt**
- *Wait for goods receipt*: invoice received but goods receipt not yet recorded in SAP, so matching cannot be confirmed.
- *Get goods receipt from WM*: waiting for or retrieving goods receipt data from WM.

**Approval**
- *Sent for approval*: forwarded to an authorised employee, manager or budget holder.
- *On hold at approver*: waiting for the approver's decision.
- *Asked for Comment*: clarification requested from another participant before a decision.
- *Approval forwarded*: approver handed the decision to someone else (more authority or closer to the transaction).
- *Returned with Comment*: sent back to an earlier stage with remarks on what to fix.
- *Approval Escalated*: no answer within the deadline, moved to a higher-level approver.
- *Approved MM*: PO-related invoice authorised in MM; discrepancies resolved or accepted.
- *Approved MM (accept if price diff)*: approved despite a price difference within tolerance or specifically authorised.
- *Approved*: business approval granted; may continue to posting subject to remaining checks.
- *Approval rejected*: approver declined (wrong amount, policy violation, missing documents, disputed charge).

**Posting** (the first three are `POSTING_ACTIVITIES` in `common.py`)
- *MM Posted Automatically*: matched all purchasing documents and passed all controls; posted with no manual work.
- *MM Autopost after manual process*: manual work fixed an issue, then MM posted automatically.
- *Posted manually*: a user posted it, typically because of exceptions or discrepancies.
- *Posting in progress*: the system is creating the accounting transaction; near completion.
- *Auto posting failed*: automatic posting hit an error; needs manual investigation.
- *Rejected FI for AP Processing*: FI could not or would not post; returned to the AP team.

**Rework and exit**
- *Restarted process*: workflow restarted after a correction, technical issue or rejection.
- *Transfered to new Sequence number*: new workflow or sequence reference, typically during reprocessing or restructuring.
- *Invoice document deleted*: removed from the workflow (entered wrongly, duplicate, cancelled or replaced).
